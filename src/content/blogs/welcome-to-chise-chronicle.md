Chise Chronicle is a place to collect longer writing, shorter notes, experiments, and half-finished questions that still deserve a home.

Some entries here will read like essays. Others will be field notes, build logs, reading fragments, or snapshots from projects still taking shape. The point is not to wait for perfect polish before publishing something worth keeping.

If you are reading the early version of the site, you are seeing the archive while it is still being built.
