Before launch, there are a few easy wins that will make the site feel fully yours.

## Replace the placeholder contact path

Decide whether you want to publish an email address, point people to GitHub, or wire up a form service for contact and subscriptions.

## Swap in your own visuals

The current build uses the theme's starter artwork. Replace the logo and cover images when you are ready for a more distinctive identity.

## Start filling the archive

Once a handful of real posts exist, the design starts feeling less like a template and more like a publication.
