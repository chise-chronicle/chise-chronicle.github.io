The plan for Chise Chronicle is simple: make a site that feels more like a living notebook than a polished brochure.

That means keeping room for drafts, revisions, references, and ideas that are still in motion. It also means building a structure that can hold very different kinds of entries without making everything feel identical.

Over time, the archive can grow into a mix of essays, logs, references, and linked themes that reward wandering around.
